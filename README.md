# ☕ Brewfully

> A quiet companion for your morning cup.

**Brewfully** is a simple, minimal coffee brewing guide built for people who want better coffee without having to think too damn much about the math.

Choose your brewing method, enter your coffee amount or number of cups, pick your preferred strength, and Brewfully handles the rest.

It calculates the water, ratio, brew time, and guides you through every phase with a built-in countdown timer.

### 🌿 Brew slowly. Brew properly.

[**Try Brewfully →**](https://brewfully.vercel.app/)

---

## What is Brewfully?

Making coffee should be relaxing.

But figuring out ratios, water measurements, bloom times, pouring phases, and total brew time can turn a peaceful morning into a tiny fucking chemistry experiment.

Brewfully keeps it simple.

You tell it what you're brewing and how much coffee you want.

Brewfully tells you what to do.

No complicated charts.  
No unnecessary settings.  
No accounts.  
No clutter.

Just coffee.

## ☕ Brewing Methods

### Pour Over
**1:16 · ~3 min**

A clean and balanced brew with a gentle bloom followed by controlled pouring.

### French Press
**1:15 · ~4 min**

A fuller, richer brew with a simple steeping process.

### Aeropress
**1:13 · ~2 min**

A fast, concentrated brew designed for a short and precise brewing process.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

### 1. Pick your method

Choose between:

- Pour Over
- French Press
- Aeropress

### 2. Tell Brewfully how much you're making

You can calculate your brew by:

- Coffee weight in grams
- Number of cups

### 3. Choose your strength

Pick the profile that fits your taste:

**Gentle · Classic · Bold**

Brewfully adjusts the effective ratio and recalculates the required water and pour amounts.

### 4. Start brewing

Once everything is calculated, hit **Begin brewing**.

Brewfully walks you through the process phase by phase with a countdown timer.

Blooming.  
Pouring.  
Steeping.  
Plunging.

You just follow along.

### 5. Enjoy the coffee

When the timer reaches the end:

> ✿ Your coffee is ready. Enjoy it slowly.
